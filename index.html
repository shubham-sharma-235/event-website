Our Story

Redefining
Luxury Events

Éclat Events was born from a singular obsession: the belief that every celebration deserves to be extraordinary. Founded in 2009 by visionary event designer Arjun Mehta, we have grown into India's most trusted name in luxury event management.

We don't just manage events — we sculpt experiences. From the fragrance in the air to the last petal on the aisle, every detail is considered, curated, and crafted with uncompromising precision.
